Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Version 1.0

TL;DR — CaramelClient does not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no telemetry, no hidden analytics, and no remote logging. Everything you do stays on your device.

Introduction

CaramelClient is a free, open-source Minecraft Bedrock launcher developed by ChimeraTeam. We built this platform to give players full control over their game without compromising their privacy. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data when you use the app and this website.

By using CaramelClient, you agree to the practices described below. Because we collect zero data, there is nothing to opt out of.

Information We Collect

Absolutely nothing. We do not collect, harvest, or sell any information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (names, email addresses, phone numbers).
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC addresses, Android Ad ID).
  • Location data (GPS or IP-derived).
  • Usage statistics or behavioral analytics.
  • Crash logs or diagnostic reports (unless you manually choose to share them in our Discord).

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address to use the launcher.

Data Stored on Your Device

For CaramelClient to function, it must save certain data locally on your device. This data is stored in the app's private sandbox directory (inaccessible to other apps) and is never uploaded to any server.

Settings & Preferences

UI themes, module toggles, keybinds, and account tokens are saved locally to remember your setup.

Imported Versions

Your Minecraft APKs are extracted and stored locally so you can launch them seamlessly.

Mods & Scripts

Your downloaded .universe, .singularity, and .so files remain strictly on your device storage.

Replay & Media

Any replay clips, screenshots, or recordings you create are saved to your local storage only.

Data Deletion: If you uninstall CaramelClient, or clear the app data through Android settings, all of the above data is permanently deleted. We hold no backups.

Network Connections

CaramelClient requires an internet connection for only a few specific tasks. Here is exactly what happens:

1. Update Checks (GitHub API)

The launcher occasionally pings the public GitHub API to check if a newer version of CaramelClient is available. This sends your IP address to GitHub (the same as visiting any GitHub page). No identifying information is attached.

2. Proximity Voice Chat (P2P)

When you enable Voice Chat, your device connects directly to other players' devices over UDP (Peer-to-Peer). The audio is encrypted and routed directly between players. It never passes through ChimeraTeam servers.

3. Caramel Network (Friends System)

If you opt-in to the Friends System, your Xbox XUID and Gamertag are hashed and stored on our secure backend to facilitate friend requests. This is entirely optional, requires your explicit action, and can be deleted at any time.

Permissions & Why We Need Them

Android requires apps to declare permissions before accessing sensitive hardware. Here is our complete list and why we use them:

  • Microphone: Required for Proximity Voice Chat. We only access the mic when you are actively in a voice channel.
  • Screen Recording (MediaProjection): Required to power the built-in Replay Mod. We only capture the screen when you press the Record button.
  • Storage (Read/Write): Required to import your Minecraft APK, read mod files, and save replay clips to your gallery.
  • Network Access: Required for update checks, the Friends System, and P2P voice chat.
  • Notifications: Optional. Used only to alert you to new releases or friend requests.

Open Source Transparency

We don't just ask you to trust us; we give you the tools to verify it. CaramelClient is fully open-source. Anyone can inspect the Kotlin, C++, and Rust code on our GitHub repository to verify that no telemetry or tracking SDKs exist.

Our build pipelines are public. You can see exactly how the APK is compiled and signed. If you find any suspicious behavior, you can open an issue or submit a pull request.

Third-Party Services

While CaramelClient itself contains no trackers, it interfaces with a few third-party platforms to function. These platforms have their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub: Hosts our source code, releases, and update manifests. (GitHub Privacy Policy)
  • Discord: Hosts our community server. If you join the Discord, Discord collects your email and IP. (Discord Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: This website loads fonts from Google's CDN. (Google Privacy Policy)

We do not integrate ad networks, tracking SDKs, or analytics platforms (like Firebase, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK).

Children's Privacy

CaramelClient does not collect any data from anyone, regardless of age. Because we collect no personal information, we are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Changes to This Policy

If we ever decide to change how we handle data (for example, if we introduce a cloud sync feature), we will update this page and notify users via our Discord. Since our code is open source, any code changes related to data collection would be publicly visible in the commit history.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or if you find a security vulnerability, please reach out:
